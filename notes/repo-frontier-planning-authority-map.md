# Repo Frontier Planning Authority Map

## Current authority

- Generic RepoModel validation owns the intrinsic frontier shape. A non-empty
  `repository_scope` is a strict lexicographically sorted set of canonical
  repository-relative paths. Every unresolved frontier routed to Eyes,
  Imagination, or Hands must carry one. Invalid scope refuses the entire keyed
  RepoModel mutation before any document is written.
- `repository_scope` is future consequence authority: the ceiling Planning may
  narrow and Hands may eventually change. Files inspected, evidence sources,
  immutable Body basis, and tool receipts remain separate reasoning and
  provenance cargo. They cannot impersonate write authority.
- Self selects one dependency-ready, unchallenged, active Imagination frontier
  from the current keyed RepoModel view. It writes one immutable
  `RepoFrontierPlanningRequest` binding the exact frontier authority document
  versions, frontier bytes and hash, repository scope, and runtime identity.
- Coordinator commit replays that request, injects the exact typed Imagination
  projection, and atomically writes a request-keyed launch binding whose hash
  covers the stored worker launch document.
- Imagination writes one immutable runtime result with an exclusive planning
  request echo and one embedded canonical `RepoFrontierPlanCandidate`.
  `safe_paths` may only narrow `repository_scope`. Imagination cannot route
  Hands or mutate RepoModel.
- The runtime derives one `RepoFrontierPlanMindRequest` from the immutable
  Imagination result and candidate hash. Coordinator commit launches one
  bounded admission-review pass serving Mind with the exact request, candidate,
  and keyed model-source documents.
- The admission reviewer writes one immutable typed Adopt, Refuse, or Hold
  judgment. Result persistence replays the request, both worker results, launch
  bindings, stored launch bytes, candidate identity, and current keyed frontier
  authority while rejecting foreign organ cargo.
- `commit_repo_frontier_plan_decision` consumes only that immutable review
  result. Refuse and Hold write one inert terminal receipt. Adopt atomically
  writes the changed keyed frontier document, specialized Mind
  review/admission, and terminal decision receipt through exact-envelope CAS.
- The dedicated Adopt transition installs the exact candidate cargo and
  provenance on `RepoFrontierItem.adopted_plan` and alone changes Imagination
  to Hands. Generic frontier writers cannot author or rewrite it.
- Self derives the Hands route from the admitted keyed model. The route exposes
  `authorized_paths`, equal to the adopted plan's `safe_paths`; Hands intent and
  Substrate Gate receipts bind that exact set. Verification receives the exact
  admitted command, checks, stop conditions, rollback steps, and consequence
  receipts.
- Modeling may incorporate Soul's verdict by changing lifecycle, gap, and
  evidence fields while preserving the adopted execution anatomy byte-for-byte.

## Derived state

- Planning readiness derives from current keyed frontier documents, current
  challenges and dependencies, and absence of a terminal decision receipt.
- `repository_scope_valid` is diagnostic projection from the shared graph
  validator. It does not own admission.
- Candidate visibility is a projection of the immutable Imagination result.
- Hands readiness and execution cargo derive only from the post-Adopt keyed
  RepoModel, never from a candidate, event, role lane, or decision shadow store.
- Completion remains Soul verdict followed by Modeling verdict incorporation;
  adoption is not completion.

## Forbidden writers and substitutions

- No aggregate model revision/hash, global thread revision, event order,
  timestamp freshness, or generic role slot owns planning or Hands readiness.
- No standalone candidate or adoption store exists in the production path.
- No caller supplies Mind's decision, rationale, timestamp, provider request,
  or strong-read set.
- No prompted reviewer may emit state, Self, RepoModel, Verification, Modeling,
  or implementation authority cargo.
- `filesInspected`, evidence references, prompt prose, namespaces, metadata, or
  “the current request” cannot substitute for `repository_scope` or exact typed
  launch bindings.
- Generic Upsert/Revise cannot create an adopted plan or perform
  Imagination-to-Hands. Verdict incorporation cannot rewrite execution anatomy.
- Same-path Hands work cannot substitute a different action, candidate, or
  command while retaining route authority.
- Mind is not a chatty embodied lane. The bounded admission-review pass serves
  Mind and has no heartbeat, lane memory, `roleAccept`, or `selfPatch`.

## Verification layer

- Hostile proofs cover stale/swapped source documents, frontier, request,
  result, job, launch binding, projection, candidate hash, escaped or unordered
  paths, failed worker output, foreign cargo, duplicate/racing launches,
  competing terminal writes, generic adoption bypass, post-adoption anatomy
  mutation, and same-path command substitution.
- Scope-specific negative proofs reject parent traversal, Windows separators
  and drive roots, duplicate or unordered paths, and unresolved routed frontier
  items with no repository ceiling. The mutation remains atomic and leaves no
  partial frontier document.
- Exact retry authenticates immutable terminal decision plus its historical
  review/admission chain without requiring later disjoint RepoModel documents
  to remain frozen.
