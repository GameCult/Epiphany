# Eyes Authority

Eyes gathers evidence outside Epiphany's resident Body. It does not own a
portable CultNet contract merely because it is an organ.

## Authority Map

- Owner: the `Eyes.frontier_research` Mind admission owner.
- Inputs: one explicit external-evidence obligation, its exact Research
  request/launch/result/decision context, and the real governed Substrate Gate
  grant plus tool intent/receipt chain used by that pass.
- Outputs: keyed Mind evidence, observation, and optional investigation
  checkpoint documents committed atomically with one
  `EpiphanyMindCommitReceipt`.
- Derived state: Research coverage is projected from the exact live or archived
  structured result and that commit receipt.
- Forbidden writers: raw retrieval results, role slots, timestamps, events, and
  synthesized evidence packets cannot accept Research or gate Modeling.
- Shared path: initial admission and post-archive re-entry use the same validated
  Mind-commit predicate.
- Cut line: source-lookup DTOs and Eyes evidence packets are absent. The grant,
  tool receipt, terminal request, structured decision, keyed Mind writes, and
  commit receipt already preserve the decision-bearing facts.

Modeling reads the resident Body directly. Eyes enters only when a current
typed obligation requires evidence from outside that Body.
